## Our church app 教會應用程式

A free app for the church: the weekly bulletin, hymnals, Sabbath School, and the
Bible in Chinese, English and Spanish. No ads, and no account needed.

教會免費應用程式：週刊、讚美詩、安息日學，以及中文、英文和西班牙文聖經。沒有廣告，不需註冊帳號。

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6816789535) ·
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.nyccsda.app) ·
[Web app](https://app.nyccsda.org)

The app is open source, and other Seventh-day Adventist churches are welcome to
reuse it: [sda-church-app](https://github.com/New-York-Chinese-Seventh-day-Adventist/sda-church-app).

Questions about the app: technology@nyccsda.org
